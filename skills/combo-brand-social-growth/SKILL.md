# 🚀 Combo 12: E-Commerce Photoshoot, Thumbnails & Viral Reels Factory

Use this growth-creative pipeline for **high-converting visual marketing assets**: commercial product photography, e-commerce listing cards, YouTube thumbnails, digital-human presenter Reels, and multi-slide social carousels.

* **Install Combo**: `.\install.ps1 -Combo brand-social-growth -Target antigravity`
* **Recommended Preset**: `.\install.ps1 -Preset kinetic-neo-brutalist -OutDir .` (or `swiss-editorial`)

## 4-Phase Execution Pipeline

### Phase 1: Hook & Thumb-Stop Engineering (`hook-mining` + `viral-hook-writer`)
* **First `1.5 Seconds` / Slide 1 Rule**: Lead with a concrete visual contrast, counter-intuitive metric, or sharp problem statement (≤ 7 words on Cover/Thumbnail).
* **Mobile Squint Test**: Every thumbnail and Slide 1 cover must remain 100% legible when scaled down to `160px` wide.

### Phase 2: Brand & Identity Lock (`logo-design-skill` + `higgsfield-brandkit`)
* Lock consistent accent color, display font, product geometry, and presenter Soul ID across all cards/slides so the feed looks like an iconic studio brand, never random AI generations.

### Phase 3: Safe-Zone Layout & Carousel Pacing
* **`9:16` Vertical Reels (`1080×1920`)**: Keep captions and graphics clear of the bottom `320px` (caption/audio UI) and right `140px` (like/share rail).
* **`4:5` Carousels (`1080×1350`)**: Use cross-slide visual continuity (panoramic elements spanning slide boundaries) to pull the thumb into swiping Slide 1 → Slide 2.

### Phase 4: Motion Polish & Multi-Platform Export (`motionmaxxing` + `ffmpeg-skill`)
* Normalize audio to `-14 LUFS`, burn or attach crisp high-contrast captions, and export platform-exact aspect ratios (`9:16`, `4:5`, `1:1`, `16:9`).
